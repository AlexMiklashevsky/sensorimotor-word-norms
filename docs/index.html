7 Rating scale

Our recommendation: the Lancaster format (Lynott et al., 2020): a 0-5 scale with 0 = "not experienced at all" and 5 = "experienced greatly", plus a "Don't know the meaning of this word" box. In particular, we recommend
  • a 0 point meaning "not at all", so raters can say that a word has no link to the dimension rather than a weak one;
  • a "don't know the word" option, so unknown words are not rated by guessing and word knowledge can be reported.
General good practice: label at least the endpoints of the scale.
